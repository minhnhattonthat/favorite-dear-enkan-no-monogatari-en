# Synopsis - Favorite Dear (PS1, 1998)

For translators, reviewers and the phase 9 story pass. Names as in `glossary.md`; honorifics per D12. Facts come
from the batches (b001-b144) and the review notes; chapter ranges are by batch and approximate (refine them when
the story pass reads each chapter).

## Premise
The world below, Infos, is threatened by a calamity (災い) driven by Apollyon and his fallen angels. Heaven
(天界) sends a young angel, the player (`{name:0080}`, gender chosen at the start, default name Raviel), to stop it.
An angel can't fight on the surface itself: it chooses heroes (勇者), visits them, gives them missions, raises
their compatibility and experience, and sends fairies (Sylphe, Shelly, Florinda, Rosa, ...) to scout and report.
The game runs week by week; the "peace level" (平和度) falls when calamities go unanswered. The archangel Gabriel
gives the angel its orders and grants Bonus AP; Michael lends the golden sword against Apollyon; Remiel keeps the
souls of fallen heroes (a hero whose soul is lost there is gone for good: "One hope for saving Infos is lost!").
Titania is the fairy queen.

## The twelve heroes (event files are per hero; most hero scenes exist once per angel gender)
- **Reiv Vincelras**: knight commander of Hebron's Voras order; stoic. His friend Regal, the Knight in Black,
  died in the last war and returns as a Knight of Hell.
- **Sievas Volkgang**: noble knight and womaniser; Adramelech burned his town 15 years ago (Tambour church).
- **Ashe Bradarik**: princess of Fangam; Sterens usurped the throne with Apollyon's power (Fangam monster);
  General Wolfram leads the royalists.
- **Tia Tangeri**: gentle girl, childlike politeness ("Mister Orcs"); the fairy-world beast Barnes (Iuvart's
  servant) and Sylphe once saved her life.
- **Beasia Gray**: tomboy swordswoman of the Gray-style dojo; her father was possessed by a fiend; Prince Milias
  seeks her out at a royal soiree.
- **Fiana Ecliya**: mercenary who sends her pay to Ellen's church; Apollyon killed her parents before her eyes;
  a crest-shaped mark brings on an illness no doctor names. Fights the slaver ring "Dark Carriage" (Zebia, Balt).
- **Yaruru Willing**: young boy with the sacred beast Rush. Rush is Jackhound, who killed Yaruru's father Ludock.
  If the (female) angel lets him, he calls her "Big Sis" (D13).
- **Ryudral Algrane**: human raised among monsters, friend of Aul, chief of the dragon clan; deals with the rogue
  Macula Dragon and the gentle monster Borunga.
- **Felimi MacDill**: his elder sister Miraya leads the Duminas army; he wants her war stopped.
- **Dian Alvis**: called "Dr. Dian" by Tia; his lost love is Liza.
- **Nasadia** ("Nasa-san" to the tavern staff): a regular who is told not to drink too much.
- **Griffin**: leader of the noble thieves Beowulf; Idavel serves him.

## Villains and reveals
- Apollyon: the enemy behind the calamities; empowers Sterens, Busik (undead), Sierra (witch).
- Gaap: chief of the fallen angels (Ptolomea, Judecca, Limbo are their places of exile).
- Iuvart (fallen angel, Barnes's master), Leraje, Adramelech (the Flame King, fiend of flame), Belphegor.
- Rasuel's double was Belphegor; the real Rasuel died in the demon world.
- Sterens is a traitor (逆臣) who usurped Fangam; Busik's crimes shame his granddaughter.
