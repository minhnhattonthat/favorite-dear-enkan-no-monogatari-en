# Synopsis - Favorite Dear: Enkan no Monogatari (PS1, 2001)

For translators, reviewers and the phase 9 story pass. Names as in `glossary.md`; honorifics per D1. This game is an
expanded re-release of game 1 (`../favorite-dear-en`, its `docs/synopsis.md`): the premise, the twelve heroes and
most scenes are game 1's, and those rows are seeded with game 1's English. What is new here: a thirteenth hero,
Juliana Andrang, and her arc (e913-e952), hero visits and talk topics (e746-e747, e923-e925, e953-e963), more
love-confession scenes, mission reports and rematch lines. Chapter ranges below are by this repo's batches
(b001-b032, only the rows not seeded from game 1).

## Premise
The world below, Infos, is threatened by a calamity (災い) driven by Apollyon and his fallen angels. Heaven
(天界) sends a young angel, the player (`{name:0080}`, gender chosen at the start, default name Raviel), to stop it.
An angel can't fight on the surface itself: it chooses heroes (勇者), visits them, gives them missions, raises
their compatibility and experience, and sends fairies (Sylphe, Shelly, Florinda, Rosa, ...; all female: "she") to
scout and report. The game runs week by week; the "peace level" (平和度) falls when calamities go unanswered. The
archangel Gabriel gives the angel its orders and grants Bonus AP; Michael lends the golden sword against Apollyon;
Remiel keeps the souls of fallen heroes (a hero whose soul is lost there is gone for good: "One hope for saving
Infos is lost!"). Titania is the fairy queen.

## The heroes (event files are per hero; most hero scenes exist once per angel gender)
- **Reiv Vincelras**: knight commander of Hebron's Voras order; stoic. His friend Regal, the Knight in Black,
  died in the last war and returns as a Knight of Hell. Held captive on Barba Island (rescue offers e904).
- **Sievas Volkgang**: noble knight and womaniser; Adramelech burned his town 15 years ago (Tambour church).
- **Ashe Bradarik**: princess of Fangam; Sterens usurped the throne with Apollyon's power (Fangam monster);
  General Wolfram leads the royalists. Prince Milias thanks her (e840).
- **Tia Tangeri**: gentle girl, childlike politeness ("Mister Orcs"); the fairy-world beast Barnes (Iuvart's
  servant) and Sylphe once saved her life.
- **Beasia Gray**: tomboy swordswoman of the Gray-style dojo; her father was possessed by a fiend; Prince Milias
  seeks her out at a royal soiree. New here: a bear's cave (e933).
- **Fiana Ecliya**: mercenary who sends her pay to Ellen's church; Apollyon killed her parents before her eyes;
  a crest-shaped mark brings on an illness no doctor names. Fights the slaver ring "Dark Carriage" (Zebia, Balt).
- **Yaruru Willing**: young boy with the sacred beast Rush. Rush is Jackhound, who killed Yaruru's father Ludock.
  If the (female) angel lets him, he calls her "Big Sis" (D13).
- **Ryudral Algrane**: human raised among monsters, friend of Aul, chief of the dragon clan; deals with the rogue
  Macula Dragon and the gentle monster Borunga.
- **Felimi MacDill**: his elder sister Miraya leads the Duminas army; he wants her war stopped.
- **Dian Alvis**: called "Dr. Dian" by Tia; his lost love is Liza.
- **Nasadia** ("Nasa-san" to the tavern staff): a regular who is told not to drink too much.
- **Griffin**: leader of the noble thieves Beowulf; Idavel serves him; Gil, Beowulf's former boss, was his teacher
  (e954). His home village is Kurumena, where his parents, killed by "that crazy lord", are buried (e607). Tia later
  remembers that she too was born in Kurumena and lost her parents when it was burned (e783-e784). Lydia is a
  hero's younger sister (e739).
- **Juliana Andrang** (new): see below.

## Juliana's arc (new in this game, e913-e952)
- **Who she is**: the daughter of the Andrang family, Kanoa nobles whose castle stands, now in ruins, near Lumia.
  Formal and composed. A fairy finds her (e913/e914, one file per angel gender); she agrees to be a hero to further
  her own journey: she is searching for her missing older brother, **Kane**. Confession scene e915.
- **The past** (e916-e920): she slips away alone (the angel may follow: a tavern where thugs corner her as she asks
  after a name, e916; somewhere she whispers "Father... Mother... And Brother is...", e917), takes the angel to the empty Andrang castle (e918). The angel walks in on her changing
  and sees a scar on her left shoulder; she claims she fell from a tree (e919). Later she tells the truth (e920):
  Kane was kind and her pride until a man named **Velk** came to the castle. Kane changed, came home one stormy
  night, quarrelled with their parents, killed them and cut Juliana: the shoulder scar is his. The servants fled
  and the castle fell to ruin. She still believes Velk deceived him and wants him back.
- **The riots** (e919, e935-e951): young people turn violent overnight (rioters of Kehle and Piscarton, the bandits of
  Oreille, looting in Gion, a monster rampage in Chirio). Juliana links them to Kane; she beats a bandit gang and
  rioters (e945, e946: "Kane's the one who brought us together"). She meets Kane (e947): he scorns his old duties
  as heir and calls himself free. At their next meeting (e948) he wavers and asks whether it is too late; Velk
  appears, goads him, and Kane turns into a monster ("Monster Kane").
- **The reveal** (e949): after the monster falls, **Velk reveals he is the fallen angel Rasuel** in disguise and
  leaves ("Until we meet again, my dear Juliana."). Kane, human again, dies in her arms asking her to be happy.
  Before that, e921 (she resolves to defeat her brother herself and asks the angel to save his soul) and e922 (she
  pushes the angel away; the angel stays). Afterwards e934: she wonders whether her brother was saved; the angel
  says yes. e952: she leaves the heroes or returns ("Juliana has returned as a hero").
- Her epilogues: e911/e912. Her battle lines: e910. Fairy reports about her: e817-e821, e835, e940-e944.

## Villains and reveals
- Apollyon: the enemy behind the calamities; empowers Sterens, Busik (undead), Sierra (witch).
- Gaap: chief of the fallen angels (Ptolomea, Judecca, Limbo are their places of exile).
- Iuvart (fallen angel, Barnes's master), Leraje, Adramelech (the Flame King, fiend of flame), Belphegor.
- Rasuel: in game 1, Rasuel's double was Belphegor and the real Rasuel died in the demon world. In this game Rasuel
  is the fallen angel behind Kane's fall, posing as Velk (e949); heroes face him in rematches (e859-e862, with
  Apollyon, Iuvart and Gaap).
- Sterens is a traitor (逆臣) who usurped Fangam; Busik's crimes shame his granddaughter.
