# Style reference (voices)

Built from the pilot batches (b001-b005, b024, b025). Add a line when a new speaker gets a settled voice.
Examples are from `trans/en/`; reuse the stock lines word for word so repeated scenes read the same.

## Stock lines (scouting scenes repeat these in every hero's file)
- （妖精の話だとこの辺りに・・・。） -> "(The fairy said he'd/she'd be around here...)"
- （私は「勇者」について、その意義、目的、必要な資質などを Xに説明した。） -> "(I explained to X what a "hero" is: the
  meaning, the purpose, the qualities it takes.)"; （彼にも理解できるよう、慎重に言葉を選びながら・・・。） -> "(I chose
  my words carefully, so that he could understand...)" (彼女 -> she).
- 実は・・・。 before the long ・・・ (the angel tells the story off-screen) -> "The truth is..." then "......".
- 勇者の資質 -> "the makings of a hero"; 行ってしまった・・・ -> "He's/She's gone..."; よかった・・・ -> "Thank goodness...".
- A long ・・・・・・・・・ row alone -> "......"; a short ・・・ -> "...".

## Voices
- The angel (spk E783): polite and plain, contractions fine ("I'd like you to become a hero."). Never gendered.
- Gabriel / the higher angels: formal, measured, few contractions (b001).
- The advisor fairy: teasing, feminine (b001).
- Reiv: terse knight, clipped sentences ("...Impossible. I have my duties to the order.").
- Sievas: aristocratic, arrogant, "Hmph" for フ, "Good grief" for やれやれ, calls the angel "you"/"self-styled angel".
- Ashe: runaway princess, bratty and bubbly ("Whaaat?", "supeeer fun"), アンタ -> "you".
- Nasadia: sultry, weary drinker; 天使サマ -> "my dear angel"; 新人の天使様 -> "my rookie angel".
- Ryudral: casual, friendly ("See ya!", "Yep", "kinda"); raised among monsters, says "the surface" for 地上.
- Dian: courteous doctor, "Good day." for 失礼; 天使さん -> "Angel".
- Felimi: timid, stammering ("W-who are you?"), self-deprecating.
- Griffin: rough thief, dropped g's and slang ("Shaddup", "ain't", "y'know"); ド派手野郎 -> "Flashy".
- Beasia: tomboy dojo-breaker, オレ voice, rough ("Here I come!!", "Damn it!!"); 親父 -> "my old man" / "Dad".
- Tia: gentle, timid, polite ("Oh dear...", "I'll do my best."); calls the angel "Angel".
- Sylphe: bratty fairy, Tia's friend ("Nooothing.", "So there! Hmph!!").
- Fiana: mercenary, money-minded, breezy ("I kno-ow!", "Sigh... Your jobs never pay, do they, Angel...").
- Yaruru: young boy, eager ("Okay! Angel, I'll do it."); 天使様 "Angel"; once the angel lets him say お姉ちゃん, every such row is "Big Sis" (D13). Honorifics on names stay (D12: "Sievas-sama", "Tia-san").
- Mission offers ({fb:..} files): each hero's accept/refuse lines exist per hero; keep the shared JP lines worded
  alike ("I'll take it on", "Try someone else", "I'll pass this time").
- Rasuel: soft-spoken former angel, calls Nasadia "Nasa".
- Maids / walk-ons with `{f7:0080}NAME：` prefixes -> "Maid: ...".

## Dev leftovers
